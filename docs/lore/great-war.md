# The Great War

The Great War is the central historical conflict behind **10924: Liberty**.

It should be treated as a morally complicated interstellar conflict rather than a clean battle between good and evil.

## Core conflict

The government pursued expansion into the stars, universal conquest, and species eradication.

The rebellion resisted that future, but the rebellion was not innocent. In desperation, it used child soldiers, dedrix-infused weapons, and mass violence.

Both sides were right at times.

Both sides were evil at times.

Via Four exists inside that contradiction.

## Government atrocities

Known government actions include:

- deploying heinous chemical weapons against rebel forces
- destroying the moon that housed rebel leadership
- pursuing species eradication and expansionist conquest
- employing inhibitors to restrict Lumen minds and prevent them from using the full extent of their brains
- branding rebel forces and dedrix weapons as terrorists while using state violence at catastrophic scale

## Rebel atrocities

Known rebel actions include:

- employing child soldiers because dedrix compatibility was stronger in children
- deploying living weapons in combat
- committing mass violence and genocide in the name of survival
- treating children like strategic assets when the war became desperate enough

## Via-class weapons

The Via program emerged from rebel attempts to counter government chemical weapons through dedrix infusion.

There were 119 attempted Via-class weapons and five complete Via-class weapons during the war.

Via Four is the only surviving complete Via-class weapon.

## Gameplay implications

The player should never feel like they are participating in a simple heroic rebellion fantasy.

Combat can be thrilling, but the world around it should keep asking what the fighting costs and who pays that cost.

Possible playable expressions include:

- battlefield survival as young Via
- high-power dedrix transformation sequences
- civilian evacuation and protection
- sabotage against government infrastructure
- encounters with propaganda and after-action reports
- moments where rebel orders are strategically necessary but morally horrifying
- inhibitor zones that disrupt transformation reliability

## Theme

The Great War is the furnace that created Via Four.

But the game should not end with the war defining her.

The deeper story is what happens when a person made by war survives long enough to ask who she wants to become.
