# Dedrix

Dedrix is the unstable transformative compound at the center of **10924: Liberty**.

It was tested by rebel scientists as a counter-agent against government chemical weapons during the Great War. In adults it proved too difficult to control and often lethal. Young children showed compatibility in varying quantities, which led to the creation and use of child soldiers.

## Narrative function

Dedrix should never be treated as only a power system.

It is medicine, weapon, trauma, body horror, survival mechanism, and identity pressure all at once.

For Via Four, dedrix is inseparable from her body and history. It gives her impossible capability, but it also marks the moment her childhood was taken and her body became strategically useful to adults.

## Transformation mechanic

Dedrix transformation should feel deliberate and procedural rather than like selecting an item from a menu.

The current design direction is a directional-input command system inspired by pressure-based call-in mechanics:

- the player holds a transformation key
- a short directional sequence is entered
- Via attempts to shape dedrix into the intended form
- stress, injury, inhibitors, or emotional state may affect reliability

The sequence should feel like Via weaving her own body into shape.

## Directional language

Directional inputs can carry internal meaning:

- **Up:** expand, rise, extend, sense, aerial movement
- **Down:** anchor, harden, stabilize, endure
- **Left:** fold inward, hide, shield, redirect
- **Right:** project, pierce, attack, advance

This lets form commands behave like a physical language rather than arbitrary cheat codes.

## Example forms

These examples are design references, not locked implementation requirements.

- **Bulwark Form:** defensive mass, civilian protection, anti-artillery posture
- **Raptor Form:** speed, aerial movement, strike-and-retreat combat
- **Needle Form:** precision penetration, sabotage, assassination, morally loaded use cases
- **Bloom Form:** sensory expansion, chemical residue detection, survivor search
- **Horror Form:** heavy combat body, high power, high fear response from observers
- **Stabilize Self:** emergency self-regulation to prevent overload or partial transformation collapse

## Progression arc

At first, dedrix happens to Via.

Then adults command it through her.

Eventually, she commands herself.

The mechanic should support that emotional progression.
