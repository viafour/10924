# Druz

Druz is the homeworld of the Druzhinzi.

The planet is defined by colossal canopy systems, deep atmospheric verticality, immense jungles, pale stone architecture, and the scar known as Kebun-Da'l.

Druz is beautiful, alive, and dangerous.

## The canopy world

Druz is not dominated by open plains or traditional fantasy kingdoms.

Much of the world is consumed by:

- massive trees
- layered canopies
- aerial civilization
- elevated terraces
- cliffs hidden beneath foliage
- deep mountain systems
- mist-filled vertical spaces

Many cliffs and mountains still rest far below the upper tree lines.

The Druzhinzi built their civilization around height, flight, and open air.

## Architecture

Druzhinzi cities often feature:

- pale marble and light stone
- circular terraces
- elevated decks
- open courtyards
- ornate railings
- layered walkways
- decorative scholarly spaces

Architecture is intended to feel elegant rather than purely practical.

Flight shapes city design.

## Society and atmosphere

The sounds of Druz are often described as:

- distant debates
- training grounds
- clashing weapons
- wind through foliage
- high aerial movement
- ceremonial gatherings

Druzhinzi society places strong emphasis on:

- knowledge
- strength
- poise
- tradition
- family
- challenge
- personal excellence

The world can feel both scholarly and militaristic simultaneously.

## Kebun-Da'l

Kebun-Da'l translates roughly to:

> death to them that lie

It is a massive planetary scar and trench system that tears across Druz.

The deepest regions of Kebun-Da'l contain creatures feared even by Kazkadarri.

Only a handful of exiles have ever been sentenced there.

At one point in Druzhinzi history, a massive extermination assault involving millions of soldiers entered Kebun-Da'l.

None returned.

The creatures remain within the crevice for reasons unknown.

Kebun-Da'l is one of the emotional and mythic anchors of the setting.

## Trival

Trival is among the major scholarly and cultural centers of Druz.

It is known for:

- massive libraries
- academic traditions
- public debate
- historical records
- intellectual prestige

Knowledge is treated as both virtue and power.

## Emotional identity

Druz should feel:

- ancient
- vertical
- elegant
- oppressive in scale
- lush
- windswept
- reflective
- quietly dangerous

The player should often feel very small within the world.
