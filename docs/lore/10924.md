# Universe 10924

10924 is a far-future science-fantasy universe shaped by deep time, ancient species, living memory, catastrophic war, and entities known as Kazkadarri.

The universe is vast, old, emotionally heavy, and fundamentally unconcerned with the individual. Beauty and horror coexist naturally.

## Current game lens

The current first-game lens is **10924: Liberty**.

Rather than beginning with Druz and the Druzhinzi, the playable prototype now begins with Via Four: a dedrix-infused child weapon created during the Great War and later registered as a sentient catastrophe-class armament.

This lens emphasizes:

- personhood under institutional classification
- state violence and rebel desperation
- transformation as both power and trauma
- liberty as a name, theme, and unresolved question
- the aftermath of surviving what others made you for

## Time spectrums

Species in 10924 exist across different perceived time spectrums.

Human Mean Time (HMT) is commonly used as a shared reference point between species.

Examples include:

- Meum line: 33% of HMT
- Geum line: 66% of HMT
- Unali line: 100% of HMT
- Iden line: 200% of HMT
- Qur line: 3500000% of HMT

Druzhinzi, Humans, Orea, Gheddal, and several others exist on the Unali line.

The Daed uniquely exist between both the Unali and Qur spectrums.

## Kazkadarri

Kazkadarri are ascended beings defined not by species, but by state of being.

Any entity may theoretically awaken as a Kazkadarri:

- people
- stars
- planets
- plants
- other forms of existence

Each Kazkadarri possesses a deeply personal directive that shapes their behavior and existence.

Kazkadarri tend to view reality less through good and evil and more through order, chaos, creation, destruction, and becoming.

## U

U is among the earliest Kazkadarri and is responsible for creating:

- Druz
- the Druzhinzi
- Kebun-Da'l
- the Daed
- additional worlds and species

U disappeared before completing several works, including the Daed species.

That absence echoes throughout the setting.

## The Great War

The Great War is a major historical wound in 10924.

It should be understood as a conflict where both government and rebellion committed atrocities. The government pursued conquest, species eradication, chemical warfare, and Lumen mind inhibition. The rebellion fought annihilation, but employed child soldiers, living weapons, and mass violence.

Via Four is one of the clearest playable expressions of that contradiction.

## Emotional themes

10924 revolves around recurring themes of:

- becoming
- memory
- isolation
- tradition
- identity
- cosmic indifference
- survival against overwhelming scale
- personhood under systems of control
- the persistence of meaning despite uncaring reality

The setting should feel ancient beyond comprehension, yet emotionally intimate in individual moments.

## Technology and fantasy

10924 intentionally blurs science fiction and fantasy.

Advanced civilizations, ancient entities, biological impossibilities, and metaphysical systems coexist naturally.

The world should not over-explain itself.

Mystery is part of the atmosphere.
