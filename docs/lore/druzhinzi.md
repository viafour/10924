# Druzhinzi

The Druzhinzi are a winged bipedal species created by the Kazkadarri U.

They are among the defining peoples of Universe 10924 and the primary inhabitants of Druz.

## Physical traits

Druzhinzi are small-framed but physically powerful.

Common characteristics include:

- hollow light bones
- dense musculature
- large feathered wings
- strong aerial agility
- multiple circulatory systems
- three hearts

Average heights:

- males: roughly 4'8" to 5'6"
- females: roughly 4'3" to 4'10"

Wing coloration varies widely, though black, brown, and cream are common.

Long exposure to sunlight and flight often produces bronze or gold skin tones.

## Longevity

Druzhinzi are one of the longest-lived species in the setting.

- average lifespan: approximately seven billion years
- physical maturity: approximately 120 years

Their immense lifespan shapes every aspect of their culture.

Tradition becomes stability in a changing universe.

## Culture

Druzhinzi culture revolves around:

- tradition
- family prestige
- strength
- knowledge
- poise
- ritual
- challenge
- scholarship

They are often reclusive and view many outside cultures as lesser.

At the same time, they maintain immense libraries, academic traditions, and formal systems of debate.

The Druzhinzi can feel simultaneously:

- scholarly
- aristocratic
- martial
- melancholic
- prideful

## Flight and identity

Flight is central to Druzhinzi identity.

Their architecture, language, traditions, combat, and social structure are all influenced by aerial life.

The Druzhinzi are apex aerial predators outside of Kebun-Da'l.

Falling is culturally and physically alien to them.

## Zhinzi language

The primary Druzhinzi language is Zhinzi.

It is composed of:

- whistles
- clicks
- clucks
- sharp vocal tones

The language was shaped for communication during flight and through heavy winds.

To outsiders it may sound harsh or loud.

To Druzhinzi it is highly nuanced and precise.

## Families and Decks

Family identity is foundational to Druzhinzi society.

The shorter a family name is, the more prestigious the family is considered.

The ruling noble houses are known as Decks.

There are twelve primary Deck families.

Among the most important are:

- Io
- Etil
- Dü
- Ran
- Wen

Deck conflicts, rivalries, and traditions can last hundreds of millions of years.

## Selection

At approximately 120 years of age, Druzhinzi infants undergo a ceremonial trial known as Selection.

Upon successful completion, they earn the right to declare their own name.

Selection often includes:

- trials of speed
- combat
- intelligence
- debate
- endurance
- artistry
- survival

Deck Etil became infamous for a survival trial involving Kebun-Da'l.

Only a handful of Etil infants ever survived the ordeal.

## Naem Etil

Naem Etil is among the few known Druzhinzi to survive Deck Etil's Selection.

Her path and exile are central to the emotional direction of the current game prototype.

## Emotional identity

The Druzhinzi should feel:

- graceful
- ancient
- proud
- emotionally restrained
- intensely traditional
- quietly lonely
- brilliant
- dangerous

They are not generic fantasy bird-people.

They are a civilization shaped by billions of years of memory, flight, hierarchy, and survival.
