# Via Four

Via Four is the central playable character of **10924: Liberty**.

She is the only surviving complete Via-class weapon created during the Great War. Her formal designation is **119-04**, where 119 marks the number of attempted Via-class weapons and 04 marks the number of actual Via-class weapons created by that point.

## Origin

Via Four's creation was accidental.

The Via program was originally developed by rebel scientists as a response to chemical weapons deployed by the government against rebel forces. Dedrix infusion was tested as a counter-agent, but proved too difficult to control in adults—with a 100% mortality rate in anyone over the age of 11.

Children between ages 1 and 11 showed greater compatibility in varying quantities.

Via's mother died from the chemical attacks. Her father, the lead scientist tied to the work, was killed when the moon hosting rebel leadership was destroyed while he was away in a meeting of top personnel.

Via herself was healthy, but during the chaos she was confused for another dying child and brought in for dedrix infusion.

When the mistake was discovered, the process could not simply be undone. The team attempted to stabilize her instead.

She survived at **71% dedrix infusion**, and incredible feat given that Via One had the highest infusion percent at 21%.

## Childhood and war

Via began fighting as early as six years old, having infusion and training done since the age of four.

This should never be framed as clean heroism. It is tragedy born from desperation. The rebellion was fighting annihilation, but it still turned children into weapons.

Via's early combat should feel terrifying, overwhelming, and adult-directed. She should not begin as a confident superhero. Dedrix initially happens to her as much as she controls it.

## Classification

After the war, Via Four is registered under the IGT Armaments Identification Act as a **Catastrophe 2A** weapon.

That classification is politically and emotionally important.

It defines her as a threat to be managed rather than a person to be understood.

## Themes

Via Four embodies the central questions of the game:

- Can a weapon become a person?
- What remains of childhood after survival becomes strategic?
- Who gets to define terrorism during a war of conquest and eradication?
- Is liberty granted, earned, claimed, or chosen?
- What does it mean to command a body that others engineered for violence?

## Gameplay role

Via is not a fixed class.

Her dedrix-infused body allows transformation into many forms. The player should experience progression as a movement from instability, fear, and imposed command toward precision, self-command, and intentional identity.
